Alturas, California

Modoc County Seat and Gateway to Northeast California

Alturas (population ~2,800) is the county seat and largest community in Modoc County. This charming small town serves as the commercial hub and gateway for exploring Northeast California's wilderness areas.

Founded in the 1870s, Alturas offers modern amenities while preserving its authentic Western character. The town provides full services for visitors including lodging, dining, shopping, and outdoor recreation access.

Things to See & Do

Modoc County Historical Museum

Explore the region's Native American heritage, pioneer history, and natural history. Features extensive collections and exhibits about Modoc County's past.

Historic Courthouse

Beautiful 1914 Classical Revival architecture in downtown Alturas. A stunning example of early 20th century civic buildings.

Niles Hotel & Saloon

Historic 1908 landmark with authentic Old West atmosphere. Step into the Old West on Bartell's Backroads.

Downtown Shopping

Browse local shops, antique stores, and Western wear. Experience small-town Main Street shopping.

Events
  • Modoc County Fair - August (rodeo, exhibits, carnival)
  • Main Street Farmers Market - Summer weekends

Getting to Alturas

Location

Alturas is located on the junction of US-395 and CA-299 in northeast California.

  • 350 miles from San Francisco (~6 hours)
  • 250 miles from Sacramento (~4.5 hours)
  • 150 miles from Reno, NV (~3 hours)
Services
