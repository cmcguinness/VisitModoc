Alturas, California

Modoc County Seat and Gateway to Northeast California

Alturas (population 2,715) is the largest town in Modoc County and the only place for serious miles in any direction with a hospital, a grocery store, gas, and lodging. Downtown is a few blocks of brick storefronts, a 1914 Classical Revival courthouse, and the Niles Hotel & Saloon — open since 1908 and still serving.

Things to See & Do

Modoc County Historical Museum

Explore the region's Native American heritage, pioneer history, and natural history. Features extensive collections and exhibits about Modoc County's past.

Historic Courthouse

Beautiful 1914 Classical Revival architecture in downtown Alturas. A stunning example of early 20th century civic buildings.

Niles Hotel & Saloon

Historic 1908 landmark with authentic Old West atmosphere. Step into the Old West on Bartell's Backroads.

Modoc County Park

Park featuring a historic Southern Pacific steam locomotive displayed under a custom timber shelter, plus a natural playground with climbing elements and slides.

Downtown Shopping

Browse local shops, antique stores, and Western wear. Experience small-town Main Street shopping.

Events

  • Alturas Farmers Market - A Modoc Harvest Certified Farmers Market held Saturday mornings, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM, in summer (typically July–October). Locally grown produce, ranch products, baked goods, and handcrafted items from Modoc County farmers, ranchers, and artisans, including Bidwell Canyon Farm. Confirm dates with Modoc Harvest.

Getting to Alturas

Location

Alturas is located on the junction of US-395 and CA-299 in northeast California.

  • 350 miles from San Francisco (~6 hours)
  • 250 miles from Sacramento (~4.5 hours)
  • 150 miles from Reno, NV (~3 hours)

Services

Alturas is your base for exploring Modoc County:
