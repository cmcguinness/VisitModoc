Bartell's Backroads in Modoc

ABC10's award-winning series explores our county

ABC10's John Bartell has driven these backroads nine separate times — from the tri-state monument where California, Oregon, and Nevada meet, to the wild horses of Surprise Valley, to a Cedarville saloon where the bartender explains why this corner of California is worth the drive. Each episode runs 4–6 minutes.

All YouTube videos on this page have captions available for viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing.

How California Got Its Shape

Visit the tri-state monument where California, Oregon, and Nevada meet. Learn the story of a 19th-century surveyor who mapped California's boundaries by hand, revealing how one person's work shaped the state's geographic borders.

View on ABC10

New Pine Creek: A Border Town with a Dual Identity

Explore New Pine Creek, a unique town straddling the California-Oregon border. Discover how a fire initially brought two communities together, but a border dispute later created an identity crisis for the settlement.

View on ABC10

Fort Bidwell: Loneliest Place in California to Get a Good Steak!

John Bartell explores Fort Bidwell, the northeastern-most town in California. This remote community showcases the dining options available in one of the state's most isolated locations, particularly highlighting the local steakhouse.

View on ABC10

Squirrel Wars! The Brutal History Behind These Hated Rodents

Discover why Modoc County relies on hunters for pest control management. This episode examines the contentious relationship between farmers and ground squirrels, highlighting the "brutal history" of conflict between agricultural interests and these crop-damaging rodents.

View on ABC10

Cedarville, California: A Bartell's Backroads Pit Stop

Cedarville is almost as far north as you can go in California. This segment showcases the tiny town in the middle of Surprise Valley, highlighting the attractions and points of interest in this remote, geographically isolated destination.

View on ABC10

Niles Hotel in Alturas: A Throwback to the Wild West

Explore the historic Niles Hotel in Alturas, featuring bullet holes in the ceiling, ghosts in the hallway, and horses in the saloon. This piece highlights how this unique establishment preserves Wild West heritage with its quirky, immersive frontier atmosphere.

View on ABC10

A Love Letter to Northern California's Cattle Country

This segment celebrates ranching culture in Modoc County, exploring how "Ranching is not just a job in Modoc County -- it's a way of life." The video highlights the deep-rooted traditions and lifestyle of cattle ranching in Northern California's rural communities.

View on ABC10

What's Not to Like About Likely, California?

This segment explores the small town of Likely, California, highlighting how "Small Town America" is still thriving in this community. The piece examines what makes this rural California town noteworthy and charming, focusing on its character and appeal as a traditional American small town.

View on ABC10

Wild Horses of Surprise Valley Will Take Your Breath Away

Just outside the little town of Cedarville is a unique band of wild mustangs roaming the wide open spaces of Surprise Valley. Photographer Jean Bilodeaux has spent years documenting this herd as they meander back and forth over the California-Nevada state line, capturing what she calls "ghosts of the past" in one of the most beautiful places on earth.

View on ABC10

About Bartell's Backroads

Bartell's Backroads is ABC10 Sacramento's award-winning travel series hosted by reporter John Bartell. The show explores interesting destinations, unique stories, and hidden gems throughout California and the Pacific Northwest.
