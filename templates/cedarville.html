Cedarville, California

Heart of Surprise Valley

Cedarville (population 437) sits at the bottom of Surprise Valley with the Warner Mountains rising behind it and Nevada's high desert ahead. It's the only real town in the valley, and your destination if you came for the hot springs, the 1880s storefronts, or the view of the Warners that hasn't changed much since the 1860s.

(Sometimes misspelled "Cedar Valley" — the correct name is Cedarville.)

Things to See & Do

Surprise Valley Hot Springs

Natural geothermal springs for soaking and relaxation. Explore Surprise Valley on Bartell's Backroads.

Warner Mountains

Towering peaks provide a dramatic backdrop for the valley. Hiking, photography, and scenic drives.

Historic Downtown

Preserved Old West buildings and authentic small-town atmosphere.

CalVada History Museum

521 Main Street. Exhibits on regional natural history, Native American culture, and pioneer life spanning the California-Nevada frontier.

Events

  • Modoc District Fair - Held each August at the Modoc District Fairgrounds in Cedarville. Rodeo, livestock shows, live music, rides, and family fun.
  • Surprise Valley Farmers Market - A Modoc Harvest Certified Farmers Market held the 2nd and 4th Saturdays, June–October, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM, on the corner of Main and Bonner in Cedarville. Surprise Valley produce, ranch goods, and handmade items from local makers, including Bidwell Canyon Farm. Confirm dates with Modoc Harvest.

Nearby Communities

  • Fort Bidwell - Historic military fort and Native American community. Explore the loneliest town
  • Lake City - Tiny community at north end of Surprise Valley
  • Eagleville - Remote ranching community with stunning views

Getting to Cedarville

Location

Cedarville is located in Surprise Valley along CA-299, near the California-Nevada border.

  • 60 miles east of Alturas via CA-299
  • Scenic drive through Warner Mountains
  • Remote location - plan accordingly

Important Notes

  • Limited cell service in Surprise Valley
  • Download maps and directions before arrival
  • Fill up on gas in Alturas
  • Stock up on supplies before heading east
  • CA-299 can close in winter - check road conditions
