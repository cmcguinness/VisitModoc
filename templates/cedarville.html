Cedarville, California

Heart of Surprise Valley

Cedarville (population ~500) is a picturesque small town nestled in Surprise Valley along the California-Nevada border. Known for natural hot springs, ranching heritage, and stunning Warner Mountain views.

This peaceful community offers a true escape with authentic small-town atmosphere, natural hot springs, and access to remote wilderness areas. Cedarville provides basic services and serves as a base for exploring Surprise Valley.

Things to See & Do

Surprise Valley Hot Springs

Natural geothermal springs for soaking and relaxation. Discover the valley on Bartell's Backroads.

Warner Mountains

Towering peaks provide a dramatic backdrop for the valley. Hiking, photography, and scenic drives.

Historic Downtown

Preserved Old West buildings and authentic small-town atmosphere.

Cedarville Museum

Local history, artifacts, and exhibits about Surprise Valley's heritage.

Nearby Communities

Getting to Cedarville

Location

Cedarville is located in Surprise Valley along CA-299, near the California-Nevada border.

  • 60 miles east of Alturas via CA-299
  • Scenic drive through Warner Mountains
  • Remote location - plan accordingly
Important Notes

  • Download maps and directions before arrival
  • Fill up on gas in Alturas
  • Stock up on supplies before heading east
