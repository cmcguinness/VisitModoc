Welcome to Modoc County, California

Experience the authentic rural charm of Northeast California, where wide-open spaces, pristine wilderness, and abundant wildlife await.

Modoc County, CA offers visitors a unique opportunity to disconnect from the busy world and reconnect with nature. Whether you're interested in exploring California's outdoor recreation or seeking a peaceful tourism destination, this corner of the state delivers authentic experiences. From the volcanic landscapes near Lava Beds National Monument to the serene beauty of mountain lakes and national forests, there's adventure around every corner in Modoc County.

This unofficial guide was created by a Modoc County enthusiast to help visitors discover the area. It is not affiliated with local government or tourism agencies.

Planning Your Visit

Modoc County offers something increasingly rare: genuine solitude and unspoiled wilderness. If you're seeking an escape from crowded tourist destinations, this is California's best-kept secret. With over 1.6 million acres of public lands, world-class fishing, abundant wildlife, and authentic small-town charm, it's perfect for nature lovers and outdoor enthusiasts who want to experience the West as it once was.

Top activities include fishing in pristine lakes and streams, hiking through Modoc National Forest, wildlife viewing (especially wild horses and pronghorn antelope), exploring the Warner Mountains, and visiting nearby Lava Beds National Monument. The county is also excellent for hunting, camping, photography, and stargazing thanks to minimal light pollution.

Summer (June-September) offers the best weather for hiking, camping, and water activities, with warm days and cool nights. Spring brings wildflowers and excellent fishing. Fall features stunning foliage and hunting season. Winter is ideal for snowmobiling and cross-country skiing. Each season offers unique experiences, so the best time depends on your interests.

Modoc County is located in the northeast corner of California. Most visitors drive, as the scenic journey is part of the experience. The closest commercial airports are in Redding, CA (2.5 hours) and Reno, NV (3 hours). From major cities: San Francisco is about 6 hours, Sacramento 4.5 hours, and Portland 5 hours.
