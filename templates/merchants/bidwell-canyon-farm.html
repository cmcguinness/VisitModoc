Bidwell Canyon Farm

Seasonal Fruits & Vegetables from the Mouth of Bidwell Canyon

About the Farm

Bidwell Canyon Farm is a family-run, ecosystem-friendly farm growing a diverse variety of seasonal fruits and vegetables for the local Surprise Valley and Modoc County community.

Tucked into the mouth of Bidwell Canyon near Fort Bidwell — California's northeastern-most town — the farm sits at the edge of the Warner Mountains in one of the state's most remote and beautiful valleys. The family acknowledges that the farm operates on the ancestral lands of the Gidutikad Band of the Northern Paiute, who stewarded this ecosystem for generations.

From spring greens to summer tomatoes, peppers, and squash, the farm's harvest reflects the rhythm of the Surprise Valley growing season — fresh, local, and rooted in place.

Where to Find Bidwell Canyon Farm

Multiple ways to enjoy the farm's harvest throughout the growing season (April–October).

Order Online

Shop the farm's seasonal produce through their GrownBy farm shop. Orders are accepted Thursday 6 PM through Friday 6 PM, with multiple pickup and delivery options across Fort Bidwell, Alturas, and Lakeview, OR.

Use promo code TAKE20 for 20% off. They accept credit cards, EBT, cash, check, and Venmo.

Farmstays & Farm Work

The farm offers farmstay accommodations and work-on-the-farm opportunities for visitors who want a deeper experience of Surprise Valley's small-farm life. Contact the farm directly to learn more.

Modoc County Farmers Markets

Bidwell Canyon Farm is a regular vendor at Modoc County's two certified farmers markets, run by the nonprofit Modoc Harvest. Everything sold at these markets is grown or made by local farmers, ranchers, and artisans.

Alturas Farmers Market

Modoc Certified Farmers Market

When: Saturday mornings, summer season (typically July–October)

Time: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Where: Downtown Alturas (check Modoc Harvest for the current location)

What you'll find: Locally grown fruits and vegetables, ranch products, baked goods, and handcrafted artisan items.

Surprise Valley Farmers Market

Cedarville

When: 2nd and 4th Saturdays, June–October

Time: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Where: Corner of Main and Bonner, Cedarville

What you'll find: Surprise Valley produce, ranch goods, and handmade items from local makers.

Schedules vary by season. Confirm current dates and locations through Modoc Harvest or by emailing FarmersMarket@ModocHarvest.org before your visit.
