The Vault

Coffee House & Artisan Goods in Historic Downtown Cedarville

Welcome to The Vault

A welcoming independent coffee house in historic downtown Cedarville, California.

Step inside for a fresh brewed cup of coffee, a cool refreshing Italian soda, or a fresh baked scone, cookie, or pastry — and settle in to enjoy the easy small-town pace.

The Vault is the kind of small-town gathering spot Surprise Valley is known for — a friendly stop for travelers exploring CA-299, motorcycle riders crossing the Warner Mountains, and locals catching up over a cup.

What's on the Menu

Fresh Brewed Coffee

Hand-poured choices of brews and flavors, plus a self-serve house coffee bar. Whether you want something carefully crafted or a quick refill, The Vault has you covered.

Espresso & Lattes

Espresso drinks made with carefully selected beans for a rich, smooth flavor. Lattes, chai, and other hot or iced specialty drinks round out the menu.

Classic Italian Sodas

Need a refreshing drink on a hot Surprise Valley afternoon? Choose from over 15 flavors of handmade Italian sodas, or mix and match your own combination.

Local Baked Goods

Fresh baked scones, cookies, and pastries — locally produced and made fresh every day. The perfect little treat to pair with your favorite drink.
