Local & Real Food on Main Street, Cedarville

Welcome to Valley Farm Store

"Making local & real food accessible to all."

Valley Farm Store is a beloved independent grocery, cafe, and gathering spot in the heart of Cedarville. Step inside for fresh meat, local produce, organic ice cream, ranch and home goods, garden seeds, espresso drinks, made-to-order food, and a warm welcome from one of Surprise Valley's most passionate champions of real food.

Whether you're picking up groceries, grabbing lunch on the road through Surprise Valley, or settling in for Friday-night pizza, the Valley Farm Store stocks and serves food worth slowing down for.

In the Store

Meat

Local and regional meat, including pasture-raised options. A go-to source for high-quality protein in Surprise Valley.

Produce

Fresh fruits and vegetables, including local greens and seasonal harvests from area farms.

Organic Ice Cream

Scoops, cones, sandwiches, milkshakes, and root beer floats. A summer-afternoon must-stop in Cedarville.

Ranch & Home Goods

Garden seeds, pantry staples, and household goods for Surprise Valley life. Plus more — the inventory rotates with the seasons.

About the menu highlights below

A Taste of Farm Store Eats

Made-to-order food served Thursday–Sunday, 11:30 AM until close. A few favorites to give you the idea:

Burrito Bowl

Your choice of meat over rice and beans, topped with cheese, tomato, lettuce, salsa, sour cream, pickled onion, and chipotle mayo.

Sourdough BLT

Uncured bacon and raw cheddar on toasted organic sourdough with organic tomato, local greens, and house-made aioli. Served with chips.

Yogurt Parfait

Alexandre A2 yogurt topped with grain-free granola, fresh fruit (or local jam), and maple syrup.

The full menu rotates with the seasons and includes other salads, sandwiches, soups, nachos, and daily specials.

Friday Night Pizza

Wood-style pies served Fridays from 4 PM, on a 10" house crust made in-house from local unbleached flour. A couple of the regulars:

The Beefy Babe

Red sauce, mozzarella, local shredded beef, pickled onion, and a local jalapeño honey drizzle.

The BBQ Bird

Red sauce, mozzarella, red onion, and pastured chicken with a BBQ sauce drizzle.

Other pizzas (and a caesar salad) are also on the Friday-night board.

From the Drink Bar

Locally roasted coffee, espresso drinks, teas, and house-made specialties. A few to look for:

Cowboy Latte

Frothed maple cream with fresh-made coffee. Iced or hot.

Cold Brew Coffee

Locally roasted coffee cold-brewed for 24+ hours. Black or topped with cream. (When available.)

Strawberry Spritzer

A refreshing sparkling strawberry spritzer for warm Surprise Valley afternoons.

Plus matcha, hot cocoa, organic lemonade, hot apple cider, loose-leaf teas, and a rotating cold case.

Sweet Treats

House-made and local-sourced. A few of the standouts:

Organic Ice Cream Cone

Scoops of vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, mint chocolate chip, or cookies & cream — with optional toppings like local jam.

Ice Cream Sandwich

A scoop of ice cream sandwiched between two house-made organic cookies. Ask about the day's combos.

Root Beer Float

Double scoop of vanilla ice cream with organic root beer.

Plus milkshakes, house-made organic cookies, and other sweets.

Our Standards

At Valley Farm Store, the team uses as many locally and regionally sourced ingredients as possible. Menu items may shift slightly with the seasons. Everything on the menu — and in the store — is free of refined seed oils, synthetic food dyes, and ultra-processed preservatives or ingredients.
