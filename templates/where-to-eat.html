Where to Eat

Local flavors and hearty meals

Modoc County offers authentic small-town dining experiences. From classic American fare to Mexican cuisine, you'll find hearty, home-style cooking that fuels your adventures.

Dining by Location

Alturas - Most Dining Options

The county seat offers the widest variety of restaurants:

  • American & Steakhouses (Niles Hotel & Saloon, Brass Rail, Johnny's)
  • Mexican cuisine (Los Potrillos, El Tepeyac)
  • Breakfast & Cafes
  • Pizza & Fast Food

Dining Tips for Visitors

Limited Hours

Many restaurants close early (8-9 PM) and some are closed on Sundays or Mondays. Call ahead to confirm hours, especially in smaller towns.

Cash Recommended

While most places accept cards, some smaller establishments may be cash-only. ATMs are available in Alturas.

Bring Snacks

If you're exploring remote areas, pack food and water. Services can be sparse, and you may be hours from the nearest restaurant.

Local Flavor

Don't expect fancy cuisine - you'll find honest, hearty food that reflects the ranching and rural character of the county.
