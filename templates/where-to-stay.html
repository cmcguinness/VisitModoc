Campgrounds & RV Parks

Modoc County offers extensive camping opportunities, from developed campgrounds to dispersed backcountry camping.

Developed Campgrounds

USFS campgrounds in Modoc National Forest:

  • Medicine Lake - Near volcanic features, boat launch
  • Hemlock - Medicine Lake area
  • Patterson - Lakeside camping and fishing
  • Reservoir C - Remote, peaceful setting
  • Stough Reservoir - Small, quiet campground

Reserve on Recreation.gov

Dispersed Camping

Free camping throughout Modoc National Forest:

  • Camp anywhere on national forest land (outside developed campgrounds)
  • No facilities - pack in/pack out
  • 14-day stay limit
  • Follow Leave No Trace principles

Tips for Your Stay

Book Ahead

Lodging options are limited in this rural county. Book well in advance, especially during hunting season (fall) and summer vacation months.

Prepare for Remote Areas

Cell service can be spotty. Download maps offline, bring paper maps, and ensure your vehicle is in good condition.
